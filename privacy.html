Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy explains what information we collect on americanstages.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

American Stages Realty & Management, Inc. (CA DRE#02094901) operates this website. You can reach us at leasing@americanstages.com or (805) 819-0911, or by mail at 3875 Constellation Rd, Suite C, Lompoc, CA 93436.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: what you give us directly, and what's generated automatically as you use the site.

  • Contact forms (Buy, Sell, Contact, Newsletter): name, email, phone, property address, and anything you write in the message field.
  • AI chat assistant ("Ask American Stages"): the text of your conversation is sent to our own server to generate a reply. No account or payment information is required.
  • Site usage data: pages visited, device/browser type, and advertising campaign parameters (UTMs), collected automatically via cookies and similar technology.

We don't collect card, bank account, or Social Security numbers through this site — we don't process payments here.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about a property, rental, or management question.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand which pages and ads are working, and to improve the site.
  • To comply with legal or real estate licensing (DRE) obligations where applicable.

Third-party services we use

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the site. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Web3Forms — processes our contact form submissions.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) and Meta Pixel — measure site usage and advertising performance.
  • Mailchimp — manages newsletter subscriptions.
  • iHomeFinder — powers the live MLS property search.
  • Google (Gemini API, Google Maps) — powers the AI chat assistant and embedded maps.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technology for analytics and advertising (via Google and Meta). You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some site features.

Your rights (California residents)

Under the CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request its deletion or correction, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share your information for targeted advertising as defined under the law. To exercise any of these rights, email us at leasing@americanstages.com.

Data security & retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is 100% secure. We keep contact information for as long as it's relevant to our relationship with you or as required by law.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
